The Survey

Just a short survey about a site you've actually camped at.

1

Find your site.

We are focused on Michigan State Parks and State Recreation areas only at this time.

2

Give us the details

As much or as little as you'd like. We'll ask about a range of topics, such as site privacy, proximity to amenities, whether there's poison ivy, or if the site is hammock friendly.

3

Snap a shot, or five.

A photo is required, it's how we know this is a real visit. Don't worry about getting it perfect, we'll guide you on what to snap when you get there.
